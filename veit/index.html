What VEIT is

An optical construction system

VEIT is not a single art lens. It is a kit for building real effects lenses from found glass, layered washers, and interchangeable printed sections. Lensbaby sells you a finished optic. VEIT sells you the build, and the character that only comes out of glass nobody designed for a camera. You bring the glass. You stack the parts. You make the lens, and you can see exactly how it works while you do.

Found glass

Magnifiers, projector optics, broken viewfinders, a curve you liked in a junk drawer. The kit is sized to build around what you find, so the look stays yours and stays unrepeatable.

You build it

Everything threads together. No metal hardware, no glue, no permanent commitment. Stack tubes, seat the glass, lock it down, then take it apart and try the next idea.

Visible construction

The system is legible by design. Washers are aperture or lock, a wedge is a tilt, a spacer is room to breathe. You are not buying a black box, you are buying the controls.

The System

Components

Every part threads. There is no metal hardware anywhere in the kit. Mounts attach to the camera, tubes hold the glass and stack to length, washers do the precise work of aperture, lock, spacing and tilt, and a shade lock finishes the stack. Combine them however the glass and the shot demand.
